Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Is CricStats Pro free to download and use? ↓
Yes! CricStats Pro is 100% free with no hidden charges, subscriptions, or paywalls for scorecard scanning.
How does the AI Scorecard Scanner work? ↓
You can take up to 5 photos of a physical scorecard or a screenshot from any cricket app. Our backend uses Google Gemini Vision and OCR engines to extract batters, bowlers, runs, and overs into structured digital match records.
Will I need to reinstall the APK when updates are released? ↓
No! CricStats Pro includes EAS Over-The-Air (OTA) updates. When new features or bug fixes are deployed, the app updates automatically in the background when connected to Wi-Fi/data.
Is my cricket match data private? ↓
Yes, your account has completely private data isolation. Other users will not see your uploaded matches, player lists, or custom team stats.
